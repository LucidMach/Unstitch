Process

Our Creative Approach

Customise

We start with a conversation about your space, your vision, and what the piece needs to do.

Design

We sketch and model the tile pattern, colourway, and scale until it's right.

Source

We match the design against our rescued material stock, prioritising what's already diverted from landfill.

Produce

Every tile is cut, finished, and interlocked by hand in our Naarm studio.

Deliver

We package, ship, and where needed, help with on-site installation.
